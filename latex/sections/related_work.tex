\section{Related Work}

\subsection{CNN-Based Segmentation}

U-Net \cite{ronneberger2015unet} established the encoder-decoder architecture with skip connections for medical image segmentation. Subsequent variants introduced attention gates \cite{oktay2018attention}, nested skip connections \cite{zhou2018unet++}, and systematic optimization \cite{isensee2021nnu}. nnU-Net \cite{isensee2021nnu} achieved state-of-the-art results through instance normalization, LeakyReLU, and careful hyperparameter tuning. While effective for local feature extraction, pure CNNs have limited receptive fields for capturing global tumor context.

\subsection{Transformer-Based Segmentation}

Vision Transformers \cite{dosovitskiy2020image} enabled global context modeling through self-attention. TransUNet \cite{chen2021transunet} combined CNN encoders with transformer layers, while UNETR \cite{hatamizadeh2022unetr} used pure transformer encoders. Swin-Unet \cite{cao2022swin} employed hierarchical shifted window attention for efficiency. However, standard self-attention has $O(N^2)$ complexity, requires large training datasets, and may lose fine-grained spatial details. AMT-UNet addresses this through hybrid CNN-transformer design with adaptive masking to focus computation on tumor-relevant regions.

\subsection{Multi-Task Learning}

Multi-task learning improves generalization through shared representations \cite{caruana1997multitask}. H-DenseUNet \cite{li2018hdenseunet} and Y-Net \cite{mehta2018ynet} explored joint segmentation and classification with shared encoders. AMT-UNet introduces ROI-guided coupling where segmentation predictions explicitly gate classification input, with gradient stopping to prevent task interference.

\subsection{Loss Functions}

Cross-entropy struggles with class imbalance. Dice loss \cite{milletari2016vnet} directly optimizes overlap coefficients, while focal loss \cite{lin2017focal} down-weights easy examples. Boundary loss \cite{kervadec2019boundary} emphasizes edge precision. AMT-UNet combines four complementary losses: Dice for region overlap, focal ($\gamma=3.0$) for imbalance, IoU (weighted $2.5\times$) for metric alignment, and boundary ($0.6\times$) for edge precision. Deep supervision at three decoder scales ($64^2$, $128^2$, $256^2$) with weight 0.3 improves gradient flow \cite{lee2015deeply}.
