\begin{abstract}
Brain tumor segmentation and classification from multi-modal MRI scans remain challenging tasks in medical image analysis due to the high variability in tumor appearance, irregular boundaries, and class imbalance. We present BrainTumNetV2, a novel hybrid architecture that combines the strengths of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformers for simultaneous tumor segmentation and grade classification. Our method employs an enhanced U-Net with residual connections and instance normalization in the encoder-decoder path, an adaptive masked transformer bottleneck for global context modeling, and a multi-task learning framework with ROI-guided classification. We introduce an ultimate combined loss function that integrates Dice loss, focal loss, IoU loss, and boundary loss with deep supervision to address the optimization challenges in multi-class segmentation. Extensive experiments on the BraTS 2020 dataset demonstrate that BrainTumNetV2 achieves competitive performance while maintaining computational efficiency.
